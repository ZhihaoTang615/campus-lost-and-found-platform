## Completion Assessment

Iteration 2 started with 14 person-days of planned work.

Only 2 person-days were counted as completed because velocity was calculated using fully completed user-story value.

The remaining 12 person-days were not counted as completed because several acceptance expectations were not fully satisfied.

## Incomplete Work

The following work remained incomplete at the end of Iteration 2:

- **US04 – Filter Items:** Location and date filters were not implemented.
- **US06 – Upload Item Photo:** Uploaded images were not displayed in the item interface.
- **US07 – Submit Claim Request:** Claim request data was not stored.
